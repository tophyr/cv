# cv

Modify the data in cv.json to match your own experience, then use this data repo alongside [cvsmith](https://github.com/tophyr/cvsmith) to generate a nice resume.

## Unsolicited Advice

Lean on AI to help you isolate your strengths. I know it sounds cliche and slop-ey, but it's not: **Don't** lean on AI to _write your resume for you_, lean on it to simply help distill patterns in your work and achievements.

Seriously! Talk to it. The way I ended up generating my CV was literally just conversationally talking to ChatGPT about my work history and projects, and then once I had braindumped everything to it I asked it to summarize things. Not only did it summarize my experience well, it pulled out several insights that I had not thought of.

Use those insights and patterns, and then rewrite the `cv.json` here **in your own words**. Doing it in your own words is important, because you need to be able to talk about it in detail. The interviewer **is** going to ask about it, and that's not something to be afraid of - that's the entire point of the CV.
